## 🔬 Research Interests | 研究方向

- **Large Language Models | 大语言模型**
- **Reinforcement Learning | 强化学习**
- **Educational Agents | 教育智能体**

> 我希望探索大语言模型与强化学习在教育场景中的结合，构建具备个性化指导、可靠推理和持续交互能力的教育 Agent，让学习支持更加智能、精准和可及。
>
> I am interested in combining large language models and reinforcement learning in educational settings. My goal is to build educational agents with personalized guidance, reliable reasoning, and continuous interaction, making learning support more intelligent, targeted, and accessible.

---

## 🌱 Long-term Vision | 长期目标

> 我希望在研究生阶段夯实大数据、机器学习与大模型相关基础，逐步探索教育 Agent 在真实学习场景中的设计与评估方法。长期来看，我希望将技术研究与实际教育需求结合，构建真正有助于学习者成长、同时具备可靠性和可解释性的智能系统。
>
> During my graduate studies, I aim to build a strong foundation in big data, machine learning, and large language models, while exploring the design and evaluation of educational agents in real learning scenarios. In the long term, I hope to connect technical research with practical educational needs and build reliable, interpretable intelligent systems that genuinely support learners.
